# Judging Scorecard

Score each axis 0–5 before recording the final demo.

## Technological Implementation
- PayPal integration is visible and meaningful.
- AI is visible and meaningful.
- provenance/safety/action architecture is non-trivial and demonstrated.
- sandbox write/reconciliation works end-to-end.

## Design
- judges can understand the case in seconds;
- provenance is inspectable without clutter;
- stale/unsafe states are obvious;
- action approval is coherent and not editable after approval.

## Potential Impact
- target user is specific;
- problem is concrete;
- demo shows why current workflows are error-prone;
- any quantitative impact claim has actual supporting data.

## Innovation / Idea
- emphasize evidence/provenance graph rather than generic AI summarization;
- emphasize deterministic safety around financial actions;
- compare against ordinary timeline/chat workflows, not against a strawman.

## Presentation
- working product appears in first 20 seconds;
- one coherent case from start to finish;
- no architecture lecture before product value is visible;
- final video <180 seconds;
- captions/readability checked on a laptop-sized player.
